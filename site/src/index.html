Stop tapping through every release by hand.

Teesri Nazar is an AI agent that tests your Android feature on a real phone and hands you pass or fail, with proof.

Quick start View on GitHub

Free and open source. Runs on your own machine.

Watch it test a feature

Plan, tap, check, prove

  1. Plans

    Turns your feature into test cases, plus the edge cases you missed.

  2. Taps

    Drives a real phone or emulator by ids, labels and text, not by guessing from screenshots.

  3. Checks

    Verifies every expected result on screen, and in your DB or Figma.

  4. Proves

    Hands you pass or fail with a screenshot for every check.

Ten heads. One arrow.

Every release hides its own Ravana. The third eye goes looking for every head.

  • Crash on empty input
  • App not responding
  • Copy drifts from Figma
  • Back skips a screen
  • State lost after reopen
  • Double submit

Why teams use it

  • Learns your app

    Every run maps the screens. Next time, goto takes it straight there.

  • You steer it live

    Watch the phone, message the agent, pause it, hand over an OTP.

  • Stays on your machine

    Open source, no telemetry, no SDK in your app.
