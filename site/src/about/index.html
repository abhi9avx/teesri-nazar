About

तीसरी नज़र means the third eye.

The name

Two eyes already look at every feature. The developer builds it and the product manager designs it. Both know what it is supposed to do, and that is exactly why they miss what it actually does on a real phone.

The third eye has no stake in the feature. It opens the app like a customer, taps through every case, tries the edge cases nobody wrote down and comes back with proof. Dev builds it, PM designs it, Teesri Nazar checks it.

Why it exists

Mobile teams test new features in one of two ways. A person taps through the app by hand, which is slow, skips edge cases and leaves nothing to run next release. Or someone writes automation, which arrives after the feature ships and breaks when the screen changes.

An AI agent can do what the manual tester does, judge what to check, and leave behind a report and a test that runs again. Teesri Nazar is that agent for Android.

How it is built

  • Open source, under the MIT license. Read every line before you point it at your app.
  • Runs on your machine. Your app’s screens stay on your computer. The dashboard listens on 127.0.0.1 only.
  • No installs. Python’s standard library, adb and Claude Code. Your app needs no SDK and no changes.
  • Secrets stay secret. PINs and OTPs are typed from .env or the dashboard and never printed, logged or saved.
  • Tested itself. Unit tests on a fake device run on every push, on Python 3.9 and 3.13.
