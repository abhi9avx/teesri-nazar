Limits and fixes

What it cannot test yet, and what to do when something goes wrong.

Limits

  • Android only, for now.
  • Flutter, WebView and canvas screens show little in the accessibility tree, so they are hard to test.
  • Jetpack Compose shows no ids unless the app sets testTagsAsResourceId. Taps fall back to labels and text.
  • Secure screens (PIN or card entry) come out black in screenshots. The element tree still works.
  • Typing is ASCII only: no ₹, Hindi or emoji, because adb types that way.
  • Screenshots show what the screen shows. A secret in a plain text field is visible in them. Use password fields.
